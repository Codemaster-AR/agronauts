# Notes



### VPD Scans
VPD stands for Vapor Pressure Deficit.
For saplings, clones, and newly rooted cuttings, a lower Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) is better, specifically in the range of 0.4 to 0.8 kPa. 
If the VPD is too high (>1.0 kPa) for a young plant, the rapid water loss causes excessive stress, wilting, and potential leaf burn.
If the VPD is too close to zero (<0.4 kPa), the plant cannot transpire enough to move nutrients, leading to stunted growth. 
To lower the Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD)—which means making the air less dry and more humid—you need to increase the Relative Humidity (RH) or decrease the temperature in your growing space. A lower VPD (typically 0.4–0.8 kPa) is ideal for clones, seedlings, and early vegetative growth. Example: Turn on the fan less often and at a little higher temperatures. Do not turn off the fan for long, so that VPD is not too low. Alternatively:
Use a Humidifier: Position an ultrasonic humidifier inside your grow tent, preferably near the air intake.
